GLOBAL NEWS TRACKER - PROBLEM STATEMENT
=======================================

PROBLEM OVERVIEW:
-----------------
The Global News Tracker addresses the critical challenge of information overload and real-time news monitoring in today's fast-paced digital world. Organizations and individuals struggle to stay informed about current events, trending topics, and breaking news while managing the overwhelming volume of information available.

SPECIFIC PROBLEMS ADDRESSED:
----------------------------

1. INFORMATION OVERLOAD
   - Problem: Thousands of news articles published daily across multiple sources
   - Impact: Users struggle to identify the most relevant and important stories
   - Solution: Automated news aggregation and intelligent filtering

2. TIME CONSTRAINTS
   - Problem: Busy professionals don't have time to read through multiple lengthy articles
   - Impact: Delayed awareness of critical developments and missed opportunities
   - Solution: AI-powered summarization reduces reading time by 80-90%

3. BIAS AND FILTER BUBBLES
   - Problem: Traditional news consumption leads to echo chambers
   - Impact: Limited perspective and potential misinformation
   - Solution: Multi-source aggregation from diverse news outlets

4. LACK OF CONTEXT
   - Problem: Individual news articles often lack broader context
   - Impact: Misunderstanding of events and their significance
   - Solution: Cross-article analysis and trend identification

5. REAL-TIME MONITORING CHALLENGES
   - Problem: Manual monitoring of news sources is time-consuming and inefficient
   - Impact: Delayed response to breaking news and emerging trends
   - Solution: Automated real-time news scraping and analysis

6. TRENDING TOPIC IDENTIFICATION
   - Problem: Difficulty identifying what topics are gaining momentum
   - Impact: Missing emerging trends and opportunities
   - Solution: Automated trend detection and visualization

7. MULTI-SOURCE INTEGRATION
   - Problem: News from different sources exists in isolation
   - Impact: Fragmented understanding of events
   - Solution: Unified platform aggregating multiple sources

8. CONTENT QUALITY VARIATION
   - Problem: News quality varies significantly across sources
   - Impact: Inconsistent information quality and reliability
   - Solution: Source validation and content quality assessment
